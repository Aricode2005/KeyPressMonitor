STEP 1 · PIN TO TOOLBAR

📌 Pin the Extension Icon

Click the 🧩 Extensions icon in the top-right corner of Chrome and click the 📌 Pin icon next to KeyPressMonitor. The toolbar icon badge shows your live WPM or key count.

STEP 2 · FLOATING MINI-HUD

🖥️ Live Stats on Every Webpage

As you type on any website, look at the bottom-right corner for the floating KP Mini-HUD showing live WPM, Keys, Vowels/Consonants (V/C), and Streak. Click KP to minimize it, or toggle it with HUD:On/Off.

STEP 3 · REFRESH / CLEAR RECORDS

↻ Reset All Stats Anytime

Want a fresh start? Click ↻ Reset directly on the floating bottom-right HUD, or click ↻ Refresh / Clear All (F1) in the popup header to wipe all counters back to zero.

STEP 4 · 3 VIEWS & THEMES

🎯 Typing Test, Biomechanics & Themes

Open the popup (or click ⛶ Full Tab) to explore:
• F5 Test: Practice Standard, Quotes, Code, or Numbers (Tab switches mode)
• F6 Bio/XP: Left/Right hand %, 8-finger load, latency & 6 badges
• F7 Theme / F8 Sound: 4 themes + mechanical key clicks

🔒 100% LOCAL & PRIVATE

All keystroke counts are processed and stored exclusively on your device inside chrome.storage.local. Password fields (<input type="password">) are automatically ignored, and zero data is ever sent to any external server.
